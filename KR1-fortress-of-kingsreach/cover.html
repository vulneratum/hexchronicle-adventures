[COVER ILLUSTRATION — full color, framed]

A storm-lashed night at sea. In the foreground, a party of adventurers — a fighter in chain mail with a sword raised, a cleric holding a blazing brass lantern high, an elf drawing a bow, and a halfling bailing frantically — rides a small rowboat climbing the face of a great wave. Pale, bloated hands reach up out of the black water toward the boat's gunwales; one drowned sailor, weed in his hair, has already hauled himself halfway aboard.

Behind them rise sheer black cliffs crowned by a grim gray fortress; far off across a bay, a white lighthouse on a rocky isle burns strangely dim. At the foot of the cliff, a dark sea-arch with a hanging chain marks the Sea Gate. Far off to the right, on a different clifftop, a second, false fire blazes red. Lightning forks across a purple-black sky. Painted in the saturated, dramatic style of early-1980s module covers, with a thin white keyline frame.

For Character Levels 1–3
A royal colony that can only be reached by sea. A coast of reefs, wrecks, and monster lairs. And in the caves of the Gullet, where the tide comes and goes, the drowned are rising.
